## 🎯 Critical Path Items

These items are critical and must be completed on time:

1. **Phase 1 - Project Setup** (Week 1-2)
   - Blocks all subsequent work
   - Must establish solid foundation

2. **Phase 2 - Core Gameplay** (Week 3-6)
   - Core mechanics must be solid
   - Everything builds on this

3. **Phase 5 - Multiplayer** (Month 4)
   - Critical for user retention
   - Complex and time-consuming

4. **Phase 15 - Optimization** (Month 13)
   - Must hit performance targets
   - Affects user experience directly

5. **Phase 17 - Testing & QA** (Months 14-15)
   - Quality gate before launch
   - No launch without passing QA

6. **Phase 19 - Launch** (Month 16)
   - Final deadline
   - All previous phases must be complete

## 📦 Release Strategy

### Soft Launch Strategy

#### Phase 1: Limited Beta (Month 15)
- **Target**: 100-500 users
- **Duration**: 2 weeks
- **Goal**: Technical validation
- **Regions**: Home country only

#### Phase 2: Regional Soft Launch (Month 16, Week 1-2)
- **Target**: 5,000-10,000 users
- **Duration**: 2 weeks
- **Goal**: Gameplay balance, retention metrics
- **Regions**: 3-5 similar markets

#### Phase 3: Extended Beta (Month 16, Week 3-4)
- **Target**: 50,000+ users
- **Duration**: 2 weeks
- **Goal**: Server stress test, monetization validation
- **Regions**: Expand to 10+ markets

### Global Launch (Month 16, Week 4)
- **Worldwide Release**
- **Marketing Campaign**
- **PR Push**
- **Community Events**

## 🚦 Risk Management Timeline

### High-Risk Periods

#### Month 1-2: Foundation Risk
- **Risk**: Poor architecture decisions
- **Mitigation**: Thorough planning, code reviews, prototyping

#### Month 4: Multiplayer Risk
- **Risk**: Networking issues, sync problems
- **Mitigation**: Early testing, Photon expertise, fallback plans

#### Month 10-12: Scope Creep Risk
- **Risk**: Adding features, delaying launch
- **Mitigation**: Strict feature lock, prioritization, cut list

#### Month 13-14: Performance Risk
- **Risk**: Cannot hit 60 FPS target
- **Mitigation**: Early profiling, optimization budget, scalable quality

#### Month 15: QA Risk
- **Risk**: Too many bugs, delayed launch
- **Mitigation**: Continuous testing, automated tests, buffer time

---

## 💡 Contingency Plans

### If Behind Schedule

#### 1 Month Behind:
- Cut nice-to-have features
- Increase team size temporarily
- Reduce scope of some features
- Parallel development where possible

#### 2 Months Behind:
- Major feature cuts (move to post-launch)
- Focus on core gameplay only
- Delay non-critical polish
- Consider staggered release

#### 3+ Months Behind:
- Reassess project scope
- Consider MVP launch
- Plan major post-launch updates
- Evaluate team and process
